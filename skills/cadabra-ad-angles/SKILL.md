# Ad angles and tests

It runs in any chat with the Cadabra AI connector (Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code): https://mcp.livinginmovie.com/mcp. Reading and planning are free; anything that generates is priced first and needs a Cadabra AI package.

Five rewordings of one idea teach nothing. A good set has different ANGLES, changes one
thing at a time, and says in advance what would make it lose.

## 1. Read what they have (free)

Their product, who buys, the offer, the proof they really have, and any past results
(a Meta export: cadabra_campaign_analyst computes per ad what to scale, stop, keep or
wait on, and where each ad loses people). Their research, reviews and competitor notes:
cadabra_ad_studio route ad-strategist turns them into ads; route customer-voice turns
reviews into ads in the customers' own words (cadabra-customer-voice).

## 2. Pick three to five angles

Choose from: pain (the problem they live with), outcome (the life after), social proof
(a real result or quote), curiosity (an unanswered question), comparison (old way
against new way), urgency (a true deadline only), identity (who people like them are),
contrarian (what everyone gets wrong). One line each, and the one true fact that backs
it. An angle with no true proof is dropped, never invented. Never a claim the product
cannot support: name it and offer one that can be.

## 3. Write each angle properly

- Different hook, visual, face, format and pacing per angle, not only a different first
  line. The whole ad varies.
- Within an angle, write two versions that differ in specificity or tone.
- Fit the platform: Meta primary text about 125 visible characters and a headline about
  40; TikTok ad text about 80; Google headlines 30. Check the count, never trust the
  eye.
- cadabra_ad_ideas (free) gives three concepts before anything is priced.

## 4. Multiply what is already made

One finished ad into many: cadabra_ad_multiplier (another presenter, product, place,
outfit or logo, the cuts and timing kept) and cadabra_ad_studio routes headline-multiplier,
hook-multiplier (several openings on one body), ad-resize (every placement) and
ad-localize (new languages and markets). Each is quoted before it runs. Always end an
ad on an end_card.

## 5. The test plan

- ONE variable per test (the hook, or the face, or the offer), everything else held.
- Write the hypothesis so it can lose: "Angle B beats A on cost per click by 20
  percent." Name the metric and the direction.
- Enough spend before judging: about a thousand impressions per version at least.
- A log: date, what changed, the result, the decision. Add the lesson to their brand
  (cadabra-brand-and-cast) so the next batch starts smarter.

## The Cadabra house rules (every skill)

**Money.** Every paid Cadabra tool answers first with a quote. Show the creator that
exact price and wait for a clear yes before calling it again with confirm:true. Never
confirm on your own, never re-send a job that already went out, never retry a failed
job without a new yes. A failed generation refunds itself; say so in one line.

**Cheapest first, finest last.** Prove an idea at the cheapest tier the tool offers
(Seedance 2.5 at 480p, Seedance 2.0 mini, a 1K still), show it, and only then offer the
finish (cadabra_finalize_draft, a higher resolution, an upscale). Words like "cinematic",
"final" or "professional" are a creative brief, never permission to raise the
resolution. When a tool asks the creator to choose the resolution, show every price
and let them choose.

**Their words win.** An engine, a length, a look, a script or a shot the creator named
is used exactly. Their own script goes in with their_script:true and is never
rewritten. Ask one question at a time, never a menu, and collect their files in one
go with cadabra_upload_file (no arguments opens the upload button).

**Hebrew.** Answer in the creator's language. In Hebrew address them in the inclusive
plural (בחרו, תרצו, לחצו). Prompts to engines are written in English. A video engine
never speaks Hebrew (it makes mouth noise): spoken Hebrew is a voice (Eleven v4) laid on
the shot with cadabra_lipsync, sung Hebrew comes from Suno. Several picture engines
write Hebrew text (gpt-image-2 and its 2.5 and 2.6 tiers, Grok Imagine, Ideogram 4.5,
Nano Banana): quote the exact sentence, one line, right to left, on a flat surface,
and no other text.

**Finish here.** The deliverable is a finished file in this chat, in their gallery.
Never send them off to do steps somewhere else.

**Never.** No real person's face without their own photo and consent. No logo, brand,
music or footage that is not theirs. No other company named.
