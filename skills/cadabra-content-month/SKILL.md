# A month of content

It runs in any chat with the Cadabra AI connector (Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code): https://mcp.livinginmovie.com/mcp. Reading and planning are free; anything that generates is priced first and needs a Cadabra AI package.

An account grows on a reason to come back: the same kind of post, in the same slot,
with the same look. A random mix of everything gives nobody a reason.

## 1. Four answers, one message

What the month is FOR (sales, bookings, reach, a launch), who watches, which networks,
and what they can show: their face, their product, their place. Add what already worked
and what flopped (their own words and posts). A plan built on what worked is theirs; a
plan without it is generic.

## 2. Choose the series, not the posts

Two or three repeating series, each with a name, a promise and a fixed slot (for
example: a Monday tip, a Wednesday before-and-after, a Friday story). Each series has:
- One format that Cadabra makes well (cadabra_viral_formats for proven shapes, a face
  talking cadabra-talking-avatar, a product film cadabra-ugc-ad, a picture series).
- One recurring element: the same face, the same colours, the same opening line, the
  same ending, so the grid and the feed look like one account (cadabra-brand-and-cast).
- A rule for the caption: hook line, one clear call to action, the same hashtags.

## 2b. Score the ideas, then choose

Give every idea a score from 1 to 10 for impact on the customer (40 percent), fit with
the brand (30), reach potential (20) and effort (10), and tag it searchable, shareable
or both. Build the month about 60 percent searchable, 30 percent shareable and 10
percent experiments. One flagship piece per week, repurposed across the networks. A
sample mix for a personal account: insight, behind the scenes, teaching, personal,
and only a little promotion.

## 3. Fill the calendar (free to plan)

cadabra_social_month with the month, the goal, the networks, the occasions that matter
(a holiday, a sale, a launch) and what worked or flopped, and with_copy when they want
the captions too. It plans and never publishes; it prices itself, so call once without
confirm and say the number. Then:
- Turn the best three posts into real ones first, so the month starts with proof.
- Make posts in batches by series (one filming day for all the talking posts), never
  one at a time.
- Leave two open days for what is happening that week.

## 4. Per post

The idea in one line, the first frame, the caption for each platform
(cadabra_social_copy: LinkedIn wants the lesson, TikTok the hook, YouTube the
searchable title), a cover in the series layout (cadabra-covers-thumbnails). Reuse one
film in several posts: the full cut, the 15 second cut, the hook, a still
(cadabra-campaign-launch).

## 5. Keep it humane

One post a day at most, human pace, never a burst. Nothing goes live from here unless
they ask; posting is the separate, confirmed step (cadabra_publish). Look at what
worked after two weeks and shift the slots, keep the series.

## The Cadabra house rules (every skill)

**Money.** Every paid Cadabra tool answers first with a quote. Show the creator that
exact price and wait for a clear yes before calling it again with confirm:true. Never
confirm on your own, never re-send a job that already went out, never retry a failed
job without a new yes. A failed generation refunds itself; say so in one line.

**Cheapest first, finest last.** Prove an idea at the cheapest tier the tool offers
(Seedance 2.5 at 480p, Seedance 2.0 mini, a 1K still), show it, and only then offer the
finish (cadabra_finalize_draft, a higher resolution, an upscale). Words like "cinematic",
"final" or "professional" are a creative brief, never permission to raise the
resolution. When a tool asks the creator to choose the resolution, show every price
and let them choose.

**Their words win.** An engine, a length, a look, a script or a shot the creator named
is used exactly. Their own script goes in with their_script:true and is never
rewritten. Ask one question at a time, never a menu, and collect their files in one
go with cadabra_upload_file (no arguments opens the upload button).

**Hebrew.** Answer in the creator's language. In Hebrew address them in the inclusive
plural (בחרו, תרצו, לחצו). Prompts to engines are written in English. A video engine
never speaks Hebrew (it makes mouth noise): spoken Hebrew is a voice (Eleven v4) laid on
the shot with cadabra_lipsync, sung Hebrew comes from Suno. Several picture engines
write Hebrew text (gpt-image-2 and its 2.5 and 2.6 tiers, Grok Imagine, Ideogram 4.5,
Nano Banana): quote the exact sentence, one line, right to left, on a flat surface,
and no other text.

**Finish here.** The deliverable is a finished file in this chat, in their gallery.
Never send them off to do steps somewhere else.

**Never.** No real person's face without their own photo and consent. No logo, brand,
music or footage that is not theirs. No other company named.
